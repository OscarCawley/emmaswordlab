Working with Emma's Word Lab

My Core Values

Compassion

I approach both you and your project with the respect you deserve. Warm, open communication is extremely important to me: I want you to feel heard, understood, and supported throughout the whole process.

Excellence

I take great pride in my work and I am deeply committed to quality. Whether you need a careful final read-through to catch any lingering errors or more in-depth support to enhance and refine your text, I put my all into everything I do.

Integrity

I understand how important your work is to you, so I work with honesty and transparency, offering thoughtful feedback and handling your work with complete confidentiality.

The Process

Before the edit...

These are the things I need to know or assess before I start work on a project:

  • The type of text (e.g. novel, CV, blog post, newsletter, website content).
  • The genre of the text, if applicable.
  • The total word count, excluding the main title but including all headings and subheadings.
  • The preferred language (e.g. British English with -ise/-ize spellings, American English, or other).
  • The target audience and/or the purpose of the text.
  • Your service expectations, preferences, and needs (e.g. proofread, edit).

These are the things we will agree on before I start work on a project:

  • The depth of editorial work required.
  • The timeline.
  • The price – see my ‘Services’ page for more information on this.
  • The number of additional passes you are entitled to.

I will always ask to see the full text (or at least a representative excerpt of the text) before I accept a project so that I can assess whether we are the right fit for each other!

During the edit...

I am highly proficient with Microsoft Word and usually use its ‘Track Changes’ feature to edit your work, allowing you to see every single one of my changes. I will also add comments to provide more context on certain decisions I have made, make insightful suggestions as to how you might improve upon your work, and ask any questions that I may have about the content. If any questions are time-sensitive, I will contact you directly for an answer.

For all projects I provide an editorial style sheet. This is a fully personalised reference document that records all the decisions I make on style, format, and spelling during the editing process. Depending on the nature of the project, I may also provide a structural edit report, a feedback report, a code sheet for a typesetter, and/or an author queries document.

I will often provide two versions of the edited text:

  1. The original piece of work with all my edits made using Track Changes, allowing you to implement them as you please.
  2. The edited piece of work with all my changes implemented, so you can easily see the final result of my work.

I am also professionally trained in markup tools for PDFs and very familiar with the ‘Suggesting’ mode in Google Docs – let me know how you would prefer to receive my edits!

After the edit...

After I have completed my work on a text, you can implement the suggested changes and any constructive feedback as you see fit. During this time, feel free to get in touch with any questions you may have – I am only an email away!

For all projects, I offer one free additional pass of the work. That is, after you have revised your work in accordance with my edits and comments, you can send your text back to me and I can review it another time. This read-through will not be as in-depth as the original, but it is carried out in order to catch any remaining errors, or even errors that have been introduced during the revision process. As a result, we can both rest assured that nothing has slipped through the cracks!
