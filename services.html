Emma's Word Lab's Services

CVs and Cover Letters

For CVs and cover letters I can help you by...

  • correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure;
  • refining content to best showcase your strengths, skills, and experience; and
  • giving personalised and constructive feedback on how your CV and/or cover letter can be more effective.

For an extra fee – £2.50 per half a page of A4 – I can also help by formatting your CV and/or cover letter to be ATS friendly, fit UK standards, demonstrate professionalism, and boost readability.

Typical rate: £0.03 per word*

Personal statements

For personal statements I can help you by…

  • correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure;
  • correcting errors in word choice without altering the intended meaning;
  • ensuring the content is clear, relevant, and flows logically; and
  • giving personalised and constructive feedback on how your personal statement can be more effective.

Typical rate: £0.025 per word*

Blog posts and articles

For blog posts and articles I can help you by…

  • correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure;
  • ensuring the content is clear, flows logically, and that the language and tone are engaging and match the target audience;
  • optimising keyword use, headings, and content structure to support search visibility, while keeping the writing natural; and
  • giving personalised and constructive feedback on how your blog post or article can be more effective.

Typical rate: £0.015 per word*

Websites

For websites I can help you by…

  • correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure;
  • refining content to be clear, concise, and consistent with the brand voice and target audience; and
  • giving personalised and constructive feedback on how your website content can be more effective and engaging.

Typical rate: £0.02 per word*

Works of fiction

For works of fiction I can help you by…

  • correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure – all while maintaining your unique voice;
  • ensuring clarity and readability at sentence level by straightening out awkward phrasing, eliminating redundancies, and improving flow; and
  • suggesting refinements to enhance how coherent and engaging your story feels – whether it is through exposition, dialogue, action, pacing, characterisation, and description.

All genres welcome.

Typical rate: £0.0075 per word*

Don’t worry if your text doesn’t fit into one of the categories above. Flexibility and adaptability are my strong suits. Armed with my knowledge, skills, and experience, I offer a general proofreading service. This applies to letters, board game rulebooks, social media content, product descriptions, emails, newsletters, presentations, flyers, reports, and more. For these texts, I look for errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, sentence structure, and word choice. I also offer a general copyediting service depending on the nature of the text and my experience in that area. As a copyeditor I can look at your text in context, ensuring it is fit for purpose, rephrasing bits and pieces where needed (while preserving your voice), making suggestions on its style, structure, and format, and providing insightful and knowledgeable guidance on how the text might be improved. When copyediting, I do not simply fix errors but enhance and refine your text.

* This is my typical rate, though it may vary depending on the depth of editorial work required and the level of intervention needed. After reviewing the text, I will provide a fixed quote before beginning any work, so everything is agreed upon in advance and there are no surprises when it comes to cost.
