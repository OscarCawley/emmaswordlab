Works of fiction

What is included as part of a proofread?

  • Proofreading – correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure.
  • Minor formatting changes – fixing problems with paragraph structure, indentation, and spacing; publication-ready formatting and typesetting are not included.
  • Consistency & continuity check – ensuring the agreement between names, dates, timelines, facts, terminology, and formatting.

Typical rate: £0.005 per word*

What is included as part of an edit?

  • Proofreading – correcting errors and inconsistencies in spelling, punctuation, grammar, word choice, and sentence structure.
  • Sentence-level refinements – straightening out awkward phrasing to improve clarity and readability, all while preserving your unique voice.
  • Minor formatting changes – fixing problems with paragraph structure, indentation, and spacing; publication-ready formatting and typesetting are not included.
  • Consistency & continuity check – ensuring the agreement between names, dates, timelines, facts, terminology, and formatting.
  • Identification of superfluous & repetitive details.
  • Structural comments – suggesting improvements to clarity, flow, and structure, such as pacing, paragraph structure, and chapter organisation.
  • Feedback on the work as a whole – commenting on the strengths of the book and where it could be made stronger in relation to exposition, dialogue, action, pacing, characterisation, plot development, or description.

Typical rate: £0.0075 per word*
